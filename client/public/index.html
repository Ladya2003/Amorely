Frequently asked questions

Is Amorely completely free?

Yes. Amorely is completely free to use — create an account, invite your partner, and enjoy chat, calendar, feed, games, and more with no paywall.

Is chat private?

Yes. Couple chat uses end-to-end encryption so only the two of you can read your messages — not even Amorely.

Do both partners need an account?

Yes. Each partner creates a free account, then one sends a partner request so you can share a private space together.

Does Amorely work for long-distance couples?

Yes. Amorely is built for couples near or far — stay close with chat, a shared feed, daily questions, games, and a photo calendar across time zones.

What can we do in Amorely?

You get encrypted chat, a shared photo feed, questions of the day, a shared pet, date ideas, days-together milestones, couple games, and a photo calendar.

Do I need to install an app?

No install required. Amorely works in the browser on phone and desktop, and you can add it to your home screen as a PWA.
